A little help

What do the four switches do?

Skip intros and Skip recaps move past those sections when the player offers a skip button. Play next episode uses an end card when the episode is ending. Keep watching answers “Are you still watching?” without changing episodes.

Why didn't it play the next episode?

AutoSkip needs a supported end card near the end and at least 30 seconds of playback in that episode. Jumping straight to the credits won't trigger it. The streaming app's own autoplay works separately.

What happens when I use Undo?

Undo returns you to before an intro or recap skip and pauses that action for the current show. Use Resume in the popup to start it again. That pause also ends after four hours or when Chrome restarts.

I updated AutoSkip, but nothing changed.

Reload AutoSkip in Chrome's extension manager, then reload your streaming tab. Open the player controls briefly so AutoSkip can read the show name.

Activity & resets

Older counts may include clicks that didn't change playback. Undo removes a count. One-time skips aren't included.
