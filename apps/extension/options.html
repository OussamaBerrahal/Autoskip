The essentials

Where AutoSkip works

Choose your streaming apps. To choose what gets skipped, open AutoSkip while watching a show.

Your choices, kept safe

Save a copy of your settings to move them to another browser or restore them later.

Activity & resets

Counts from older versions may include clicks that didn't change playback. Undo removes the skip from your count. One-time skips aren't included.

Start fresh

Remove your saved choices, prompt history and counts. Save a backup first if you'd like to keep them.

Troubleshooting

Adds messages to the browser's console to help investigate a problem. Nothing is sent to us.

Undo also pauses that action for the current show. Select Resume in the popup to start it again. Pauses end when Chrome restarts or after four hours.

No account. No ads. No tracking.
Your preferences and counts stay in this browser. Backups are downloaded to your device.
